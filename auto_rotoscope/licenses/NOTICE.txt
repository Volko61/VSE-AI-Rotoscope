Third-party components bundled with Auto Rotoscope VSE
======================================================

The add-on's own source code is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. The full text is in
GPL-3.0.txt in this directory.

The components below are redistributed inside the built extension. All are
GPL-compatible. Their full license texts are included here.

1. SAM 2.1 model weights (ONNX graphs)
   Files:    models/sam2.1_hiera_tiny/{encoder,decoder}.onnx
   Upstream: Meta Platforms, Inc. — "Segment Anything Model 2"
             https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam2
   ONNX export: https://huggingface.co/vietanhdev/segment-anything-2.1-onnx-models
   Copyright: 2024 Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates
   License:  Apache-2.0  →  Apache-2.0.txt

2. onnxruntime (Linux) / onnxruntime-directml (Windows)
   Files:    wheels/onnxruntime*.whl
   Upstream: Microsoft Corporation
             https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
   License:  MIT  →  onnxruntime-LICENSE.txt
   Its own bundled dependencies: onnxruntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt

3. opencv-python-headless
   Files:    wheels/opencv_python_headless*.whl
   Upstream: OpenCV project; PyPI packaging by Olli-Pekka Heinisuo
             https://github.com/opencv/opencv-python
   License:  Apache-2.0 (OpenCV 4.x) → Apache-2.0.txt
             MIT (the packaging itself) → opencv-python-LICENSE.txt
   Its own bundled dependencies: opencv-python-LICENSE-3RD-PARTY.txt

numpy is provided by Blender and is therefore not bundled.

No component is modified: the wheels are unaltered PyPI artifacts and the ONNX
graphs are unaltered exports.
